Short bio

I am an associate professor in computer science at the University of Rennes in France, where I teach at IUT Lannion. I am a member of the BARD research team of the IRISA laboratory. My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence and education: I design and study learning environments and AI featured systems, and I investigate how learners appropriate them in educational settings.

Research interests

  • Artificial Intelligence for Education
  • Learning Analytics
  • Serious Games
  • Computer Science Education
