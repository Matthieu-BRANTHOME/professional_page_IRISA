I am an Associate Professor (maître de conférences) in Computer Science at the University of Rennes, teaching at IUT Lannion and conducting my research within the IRISA laboratory, BARD team.

My work sits at the intersection of computer science education and artificial intelligence. I design and study learning environments — in particular serious games — that help learners take their first steps in text-based programming, and I investigate how AI can produce pedagogically sound, adaptive feedback at scale in real classrooms. My PhD, defended in 2023, examined the transition from block-based to text-based programming between middle school and high school, and produced Pyrates, a serious game now played more than 390,000 times.

Feel free to get in touch if you are interested in a collaboration, or if you would like more information about my work.
