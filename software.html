Pyrates

Pyrates is a serious game designed to introduce high school students to Python programming. It involves the fundamental concepts of programming (conditional, variable, loops, etc.) through the control of a character in a platform game.

A range of available educational content (programming memo) helps players transition from Scratch block language. The application includes an online teacher's guide with startup tips and level solutions.
