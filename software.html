Pyrates

A serious game designed to introduce high school students to Python programming.

Players control a pirate through a series of platform levels by writing Python code. Each level introduces a fundamental construct — variables, conditionals, loops — and the level design is built so that learners who arrive from Scratch can map what they already know onto text-based syntax. The game is accompanied by learning resources and by an online teacher's guide containing classroom implementation advice and solutions to every level.

Pyrates is also a research instrument: the interaction traces it collects support my work on learning analytics and on automatically generated adaptive feedback.
