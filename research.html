Research themes

  • Artificial Intelligence for Education
  • Learning Analytics
  • Serious Games
  • Computer Science Education

My research investigates how learners acquire programming skills, and how digital environments can support that process. Three questions run through my work: how to ease the transition from block-based to text-based programming; how to model what learners do from the traces they leave in a learning environment; and how to turn those models into feedback that is both automatically generated and pedagogically sound.

This work is grounded in the field. The Pyrates serious game serves as both a research instrument and a resource used in secondary school classrooms, which allows hypotheses about feedback and adaptation to be tested with real students under real conditions rather than in the laboratory alone.
